ARVEX / PROCESS

FROM IDEA
TO GARMENT.

A straightforward workflow designed around your requirements.

01

DESIGN & REQUIREMENTS

Send your design, reference photos, tech pack or idea. Tell us the product, quantity, fabric, GSM, colours and branding requirements.

02

FABRIC & DEVELOPMENT

We discuss fabric composition, weight, hand feel, construction and finishing so the garment matches the intended result.

03

SAMPLE

A sample can be developed for fit, measurements, construction, print placement and overall appearance before bulk production.

04

APPROVAL

Once the required details are confirmed, production specifications are finalized.

05

BULK PRODUCTION

Manufacturing begins with cutting, stitching, decoration, finishing and packaging according to the approved specifications.

06

QUALITY CONTROL & SHIPPING

Finished garments are checked before packing and dispatch. Shipping arrangements are then coordinated for your order.
